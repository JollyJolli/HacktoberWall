# 2026 visual asset credits

The following premade raster assets are used by the 2026 page. Resized/derived copies retain their source license.

- Dark cemetery background (`halloween-background.webp`): Code Inferno Games, “3 Halloween Game Backgrounds,” CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/3-halloween-game-backgrounds. Credit is optional; retained for traceability.
- Light cemetery background (`chambers-mount-auburn.webp`): Thomas Chambers, *Mount Auburn Cemetery*, National Gallery of Art Open Access, public domain. Source: https://www.nga.gov/artworks/45641-mount-auburn-cemetery. Courtesy credit.
- Gravestone (`gravestone_round.png`), bat (`bat.png`), and ghost (`ghost.png`): LumioLab, “Halloween Props & FX — Free Sampler,” CC BY 4.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/halloween-props-fx-free-sampler-pumpkin-gravestone-ghost-bat. Required credit: **LumioLab — https://lumiolab.itch.io**.
- Spider sprite (`plat_spider_spritesheet.png`): ashuuya, “2D Platformer Enemies,” CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/2d-platformer-enemies. Credit optional.
- Corner webs (`cornerweb_tl.png`, `cornerweb_bl.png`): Christina Lee / electronsandsuch, “2D Spider Webs,” CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/2d-spider-webs. Credit optional and appreciated.
- Pumpkin sprites (`pumpkin_yellow_eyes.png`, `pumpkin_dark_eyes.png`): GreyFrogGames, “Halloween Pumpkin,” CC0. These 64×64 sprites are resized derivatives of the source PNGs. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/halloween-pumpkin-1.
